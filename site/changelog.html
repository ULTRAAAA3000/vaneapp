Changelog

What’s new in Vane.

Every feature and fix as it ships, newest first. This is the build so far.

Nov 2, 2026
  • ProPriority dispatch: paid dashboard plans get a lane of their own, ahead of the shared queue, and a scheduled post leaves on the second you chose instead of the next whole minute.
  • ConnectorsFour more tools for Claude and Notion on Pro: analyse how your posts did, find the best free times, read your tone of voice, and schedule a whole series of up to 14 posts in one call (with a dry run first). Pro also has room for up to 10 keys, the Free Trial for 3.
Oct 22 – Nov 1, 2026
  • PaymentsPay for a plan by card through Monobank; the plan switches on when the payment is confirmed, and you get a receipt by email.
  • ConnectorsNotion and an Obsidian plugin next to Claude; Claude can also post right away with “post now”.
  • PostingA Post now button next to Schedule post (it goes out within about a minute), and a Collapse button for the posts list.
  • SharingShare preview: a public page for any sent post, with the networks it went to, a Try Vane Free button, and a way to stop sharing.
  • DashboardOnboarding checklist, a Live results card (time saved, delivery rate), choose a file from the library in the editor, an Agent log terminal under API access, and a Feedback and Roadmap board where you vote for what comes next.
  • PinterestPinterest channel for pins with an image and a board; it switches on once the network approves Vane.
  • StatusA live system health and delivery-speed badge.
  • FixVane Studio no longer floods the server with requests after pressing Announce; Notion, Obsidian, Make and Agents tabs showed nothing and now open.
Oct 21, 2026
  • LandingThe hero video now plays forward and back, so the loop has no cut.
Oct 20, 2026
  • API protectionThe whole API now sits behind a shield: per-address limits, blocking of attack tools and scanners, automatic bans for repeated probing, and limits on wrong keys and sign-up floods.
  • StudioA network-agnostic publishing queue: pick your channels in one Publish dialog (title, description, privacy) and Studio publishes in the background with retries.
  • LegalPrivacy Policy, Terms of Service and a data-deletion page.
  • LandingA Follow section with Vane’s own social networks.
Oct 19, 2026
  • LinkedIn and ThreadsConnect with the networks’ own sign-in, publish text and one image, and get a Reconnect prompt before a 60-day sign-in runs out. Switched on when each network approves Vane.
  • WebhooksSigned post.published and post.failed events with retries, and instant triggers for Zapier and Make.
  • StudioVideo previews and length, public links you can create and remove, a Share dialog, and a one-click “announce this video” in your Vane Post channels. The Channels screen shows YouTube, TikTok and Instagram Reels as Soon.
  • ConnectorsZapier app, WordPress plugin and a Claude (MCP) connector.
  • NavigationA Post ↔ Studio hand-off card under the greeting.
  • FixConnecting a Mastodon server no longer fails with “Request failed (502)”.
Oct 18, 2026
  • Vane StudioThe video engine and dashboard: resumable uploads, a library, separate cvid_ keys, on studio. and post. subdomains with one shared sign-in.
  • MastodonConnect any Mastodon server and publish.
  • APIIdempotency-Key for retry-safe posting and the OpenAPI spec.
  • HistoryOpen a sent post on its network straight from the history list.
  • EditorA progress ring on the counter, an emoji picker, a week strip, a “your usual time” chip and a streak badge.
Oct 17, 2026
  • BlueskyConnect with an app password and publish text, links, tags and one image.
  • APIPublic API, part 1 and 2: keys, plans, quotas and a keys screen in the dashboard with a quota meter and quick start. A Make custom app.
  • EditorHashtag memory that suggests the tags you used before, an Insights card, draft autosave, Reuse and Ctrl/⌘ + Enter to schedule.
  • AdminAn admin panel with users, plans and prices, traffic, Search Console and IP blocking.
  • Sign-inA “Copy code” button in the sign-in email.
Oct 16, 2026
  • SecurityCloudflare Turnstile on sign-in and sign-up, and a 6-digit email code for new accounts and for returns after a few days away.
  • LandingNew hero and an iPad demo of publishing a video.
Oct 15, 2026
  • TelegramTwo ways to connect: Quick Connect with the Vane bot (with proof you own the channel) or your own bot.
  • MediaNative image and video upload with progress and preview.
  • BrandThe new Vane logo.
Oct 12–14, 2026
  • The startWaitlist, landing page, accounts and sessions, the dashboard, encrypted credentials and the cron worker that publishes on time.
