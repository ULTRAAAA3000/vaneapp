Integrations

Post from wherever you already work.

Ask Claude or a Notion agent to schedule it, post from a note in Obsidian, drop it into an automation, or publish from your CMS. Every connector below talks to the same Vane API, so your plan, quota and channels are the same everywhere.

● API access opens with launch. Join the waitlist to get early keys. Start with a cpost_test_… key: everything works, nothing is posted or counted.

Claude

Self-serve

Tell Claude “post this to Telegram and Bluesky tomorrow at 9:00”. A connector built on the Model Context Protocol gives it two tools: list your channels and create a post.

  1. Create a key in the dashboard.
  2. Add the Vane connector to Claude Desktop (or the remote endpoint /api/v1/mcp).
  3. Ask.

Notion

Self-serve

Keep your content calendar in Notion and let a Notion agent publish it. Say “schedule every row marked Ready” and the agent shows each post, waits for your yes, then schedules it through Vane. It uses the same Model Context Protocol connector as Claude.

  1. In Notion, allow custom MCP servers (Settings → Connections).
  2. Add https://vaneapp.dev/api/v1/mcp as a custom connection of your agent and press Allow on Vane’s page.
  3. Ask.

ChatGPT

Self-serve

A Custom GPT that lists your channels, shows you the text, channels and time, waits for your “yes”, then schedules the post. No code.

  1. Create a key in the dashboard.
  2. In ChatGPT, create a GPT and import https://vaneapp.dev/api/v1/openapi.json as its Action.
  3. Paste the key as the Bearer token.

Obsidian

Self-serve

Vane for Obsidian posts the open note, or the text you selected, to your channels now or at a time you pick. Markdown is turned into clean post text first, and you can edit it before it goes. Works on desktop and mobile.

Download the plugin → then unzip it into your vault’s .obsidian/plugins folder and add your key.

WordPress

Self-serve

Vane for WordPress adds a small Vane dashboard to your admin menu: write a post, pick channels, post now or schedule, see the queue and history. It also cross-posts every new article from the editor sidebar.

Install it in two minutes → (the WordPress.org directory listing follows after review).
