What an autoposter does

You write a post once. The autoposter keeps it, fits it to the limits of each network and publishes it on schedule, so you do not have to be online at the moment of posting. Vane also keeps a calendar, a media library and built-in AI tools, and it can be driven by an API.

What to compare when you want a cheap autoposter

Flat price or price per channel

Some schedulers charge for every connected account. Vane charges one flat price, so ten channels cost the same as two.

What happens at the limit

Vane posts inside your monthly quota. Extra posts are paid per post from a prepaid balance, only if you switch that on. Otherwise posting stops and nothing is charged by itself.

The networks you really use

Vane posts to Telegram, Bluesky, Mastodon, Discord, Slack, Dev.to and Hashnode today. LinkedIn, Threads, Reddit, Facebook Pages, YouTube and Instagram Reels open as each network approves Vane.

Automation

A REST API with an OpenAPI spec, an MCP connector for Claude and Notion, Make, Zapier, a WordPress plugin and an Obsidian plugin all use the same Vane account.

Questions

What should I look at to find a cheap autoposter?

Look at how the price grows. Some schedulers charge for every connected account, so the bill rises with each channel. Vane charges one flat price: the Pro plan covers unlimited channels, and the Free Trial needs no card.

Does Vane charge per channel?

No. There are no per-channel fees on any plan. You pay one flat price for the plan, and only posts beyond your monthly quota are billed per post, if you switch that on.

Can I try Vane without a card?

Yes. The Free Trial has 30 posts in total and up to 2 channels, and it needs no card.

Can I schedule posts through an API or from Claude?

Yes. Vane has a REST API with an OpenAPI spec, an MCP connector for Claude and Notion, and works with Make, Zapier, WordPress and Obsidian.
