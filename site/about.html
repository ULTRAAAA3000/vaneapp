About

A scheduler for people who would rather ship than configure.

Most social tools charge for every channel, bury the one thing you came for under settings, and treat an API as an upsell. Vane is the opposite: write once, preview honestly, publish everywhere, one flat price.

One flat price

No per-channel fees on any plan. Do the maths yourself on the pricing section; we will not move the goalposts.

Fewer knobs

Writing, picking networks, picking a time. That is the product. Everything else gets out of your way.

Honest previews

You see the post the way each network will draw it, with that network’s real limits, before it is live.

API-first

Anything the dashboard does for posts, a script, an AI assistant or an automation can do too. See the API.

Private by default

Sign-ins are encrypted, keys are hashed, there is no advertising and no selling of data. How we protect it.

Yours to leave

Disconnect, revoke and delete whenever you like. Here is how.

How it is built

Vane runs at the edge on Cloudflare: Pages and Functions for the sites and the API, D1 for data, R2 for pictures and video, and a scheduled worker that publishes at the minute you chose. That keeps it fast everywhere and lets the price stay low.

Two products share one account: Vane Post for text and image posts, and Vane Studio for video. The API, the AI helpers and the connectors sit on top of both.
