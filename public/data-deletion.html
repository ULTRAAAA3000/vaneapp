You decide what Vane keeps. There are three ways to delete data, from the smallest to the largest.

1. Remove one connected account

In the dashboard, open Channels and press the bin next to the account. This deletes its saved sign-in and every post still scheduled for it. Posts already published stay on the network, where you can delete them yourself.

2. Remove Vane from LinkedIn or Threads

  • LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services → Vane → Remove.
  • Threads: Account → Website permissions (or Settings → Apps and websites) → Vane → Remove. Threads then tells Vane, and Vane deletes that Threads connection and its posts. If you used Threads’ “delete data” option, you get a confirmation code, and this page shows it.

Removing the permission makes the connection stop working immediately, whatever Vane does next.

3. Delete your whole Vane account

Write from the email address of the account to [contact email] with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account, its channels and sign-in tokens, posts, uploaded files and logs, and confirm by email, within 30 days.

See the privacy policy for what Vane keeps and why.
