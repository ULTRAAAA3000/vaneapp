1. Who we are

Vane is a service that schedules and publishes your posts and videos to the social networks you connect. It is made of Vane Post (text and image posts), Vane Studio (video publishing), the Vane API with its connectors (Make, Zapier, ChatGPT, Claude, WordPress) and the AI features inside them. This policy covers all of them and our websites.

The controller of your personal data is [OPERATOR NAME], [OPERATOR ADDRESS] ([REGISTRATION / VAT NUMBER, if any]), referred to as “Vane”, “we” or “us”. You can reach us at the contact form.

2. The short version

  • We collect what is needed to run your account and to publish what you schedule, and nothing for advertising.
  • Your social network sign-ins are stored encrypted. API keys and sessions are stored only as hashes. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • We ask each network for the smallest permission that lets us publish for you. We do not read your feed, messages, followers or connections.
  • We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and do not use your content to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect any network, revoke any key, delete any post, and ask us to delete everything. See your rights and data deletion.

3. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email address, an optional name, your time zone and a salted hash of your password. We also email you one-time codes to confirm sign-up and new sign-ins. If you sign in with GitHub or Google, we receive your name, your verified email address and the provider’s id for your account, and nothing else (no repositories, contacts, files or mail).
  • Content: the text, pictures, videos, thumbnails and drafts you write or upload, the time you chose to publish, and the results (sent, failed, the link to the published post). We also keep what you save on purpose: up to 5 unfinished drafts and your snippets (reusable texts; 5 on the Free Trial, up to 100 on paid plans).
  • AI history: the last 10 answers of the post and week-plan generators, each with the idea you typed to get it. You can pin up to 4 of them and delete any of them.
  • Email preferences: if you use the unsubscribe link in one of our emails, we keep your email address on a list so that we do not write to you again.
  • Connected accounts: a sign-in token or app password for each network you connect, kept encrypted, plus the public name, handle or id of the account so you can tell them apart.
  • API keys and webhooks: the name you give a key, its last four characters and a hash of it (never the key itself); the address of each webhook and its signing secret, kept encrypted.
  • Billing: your plan and its status, payment references and amounts. Card numbers are entered on our payment provider’s page and never reach us. For on-chain payments we see the public wallet address and transaction id.
  • Messages: what you write to us through the contact form or by email (your name, email address and message), and your email if you join the waitlist. The contact form also records your IP address to stop abuse.

Information collected automatically

  • Security records: for sign-in and sign-up attempts, the time, result, email entered, IP address, approximate location (country, city), network (ASN) and browser details. We also keep short-lived counters per IP address to stop floods, scanning and password guessing, and we may block addresses that abuse the service.
  • Usage: counters of posts, API calls and AI credits used against your plan, and a log of what the service did for you (for example “post sent to LinkedIn”).
  • Website analytics (our own, no third parties): pages opened, time on page, referring site, campaign tag, device type, approximate location, and a salted hash of your IP address (not the address). It is switched off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. When you sign up we also keep where you first came from (referrer and landing page).

Information from the networks you connect

Only what the permission you grant allows. For example: LinkedIn gives us your name and member id; Threads your username and id; Bluesky your handle; Mastodon your account on your server; Telegram the channel or group where our bot is an administrator; YouTube the channel you upload to; and, when available, TikTok and Instagram the account you publish to. Each network also tells us whether a post was accepted, and its id or link.

4. How we use it, and why we may

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the legal bases are in brackets.

  • To run the service: create your account, publish at the times you chose, store your media, apply your plan’s limits, send you sign-in codes and service messages. (Contract.)
  • To take payment and keep records: process subscriptions and usage charges, prevent fraud, meet tax and accounting duties. (Contract; legal obligation.)
  • To keep content within our rules: text, pictures and a few frames of videos are checked automatically for obscene language, adult (18+) material and graphic violence before they are published, and what fails is refused. (Legitimate interests in keeping the service lawful and safe; contract.)
  • To keep Vane safe: detect bots, abuse, password guessing and scanning, enforce our Terms, and investigate incidents. (Legitimate interests in securing the service and our users.)
  • To improve Vane: understand, in aggregate, which pages and features are used. (Legitimate interests; you can opt out with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.)
  • To answer you and give support. (Contract; legitimate interests.)
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests. (Legal obligation.)

We do not make decisions about you that have legal effects by automated means, and we do not build advertising profiles.

6. AI features

When you use an AI feature (hashtags, clean-up, re-formatting, post and idea generation, tone, hooks, video scripts and metadata), the text you submit and your instruction are sent to the AI model running on our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare Workers AI, to produce the answer. The model is chosen by the language of your text: Cloudflare Workers AI runs open models from providers such as Meta and Google on its own infrastructure. We keep a count of the credits you use, and the last 10 post and week-plan answers with the idea behind them so that you can find them again (see section 3); we do not keep your prompts for any other purpose. We do not use your content to train or fine-tune models, and we do not send it to AI providers other than the ones listed in section 8. AI output can be wrong or unsuitable: you review it before anything is published.

7. API, webhooks and connectors

If you connect Make, Zapier, a custom GPT, Claude (MCP), the WordPress plugin or your own scripts with a Vane API key, those services send requests to Vane on your behalf and receive what the API returns (your channel list, post status, the posts they create). Their handling of data is governed by their own policies; revoke the key in Vane to cut the connection. The Claude connector signs you in at vaneapp.dev/connect: Claude receives an access token (stored by Vane only as a hash), can list your channels and schedule posts for you, and never sees your passwords, billing details or channel credentials; the API key behind it is kept encrypted and ends the moment you revoke it. If you add a webhook, Vane sends the events you chose (for example that a post was published or failed) to the address you gave, signed so you can verify them; you are responsible for that endpoint and for what it does with the data.

11. How we protect it

  • Everything travels over HTTPS. Social network tokens and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • API keys are shown to you once and stored only as SHA-256 hashes; passwords are stored as salted hashes; sign-in sessions are stored as hashes and sent in a secure, HttpOnly cookie.
  • The API is protected by bot detection, per-address and per-key rate limits, automatic blocking of abusive addresses, and signature checks on calls from payment providers and social networks.
  • Database queries are parameterized, production secrets are kept in a secrets store and never logged, and access to administration is restricted and logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires, without undue delay.

13. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the UK GDPR) you can ask us to: give you access to your data and a copy of it; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to some uses; and move it to another service. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

  • Yourself, in the dashboard: remove channels, revoke API keys and webhooks, delete posts, videos and public links.
  • By email: write to the contact form from the address of your account. To protect you we may ask you to confirm your identity. We answer within 30 days, and tell you if we need more time or cannot do what you ask and why.
  • Delete everything: see Data deletion.

15. Children

Vane is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. When you create an account we ask for your date of birth and refuse the sign-up if you are under 16. We use the date only for that check and do not store it; we keep just a note that you confirmed you are old enough, and the day you did. If you think a child has given us personal data, write to the contact form and we will delete it.
