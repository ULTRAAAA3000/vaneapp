FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the $12 price really flat? +

Yes. $12 a month covers all connected channels and all teammates. There are no per-channel or per-seat add-ons.

Which channels are supported? +

Telegram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn and webhooks at launch. Webhooks let you push a post to any other tool you run.

How accurate is the scheduling? +

Posts are scheduled to the minute. A scheduler on Cloudflare’s edge checks for due posts every minute and sends them straight away, so there is no extra queue delay on our side. Each platform’s own API response time still applies.

When does Vane launch? +

We are onboarding people from the waitlist first. Join with your email and we will write to you when your spot opens.

What happens to my email? +

It is stored in our database and used only to tell you about Vane’s launch. We don’t sell or share it.

Is the demo above real? +

It is a simulation that runs in your browser to show how previews work. It does not post anywhere, and your text is not sent to a server.
