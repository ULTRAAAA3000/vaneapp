FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the price really flat? +

Yes. Pro is $9.99 a month (Early Bird) or $12 standard, or $99 a year, and it covers unlimited channels and workspaces. There are no per-channel add-ons. Only posts beyond your monthly quota are billed, at the overage rates in the pricing section.

Do I need a card to try Vane? +

No. The Free Trial needs no card and includes 30 posts in total, up to 2 channels and 10 AI credits. When the 30 posts are used, publishing stops until you choose a plan. We never charge you automatically.

What happens if I go over my monthly quota? +

Extra posts are billed per post. On Pro that is $0.015 per post and $0.045 per AI-generated post. On the Developer API plan it is $0.03 and $0.09. Pro includes 1,000 posts a month and the Developer API includes 180.

Which networks are supported? +

LinkedIn, Threads, Telegram and Bluesky at launch. Each one gets its own preview and its own character limit (LinkedIn 3,000, Threads 500, Bluesky 300, Telegram 4,096), so you see what fits before you schedule.

What is Vane AI Pass? +

A set of AI tools included with Pro: a re-formatter that turns Telegram long-reads into threads and LinkedIn posts, a post and idea generator, a tone fixer, and a hook and CTA generator. Pro has a soft limit of about 250 generations a month. Hashtags, emoji and grammar fixes and the character limit guard are free on every plan.

Can I use Vane from code or no-code tools? +

Yes. The REST API comes with the Developer plan or as a +$2.99 add-on to Pro. Make.com, Zapier, a Custom GPT action and a Claude MCP server are planned right after launch.

When will video publishing arrive? +

The Video Engine ($8.99 a month) is the next expansion, covering YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube long-form. It needs approval from each platform before it can go live, so we will announce the date once those clear. Join the waitlist and we will tell you.

How do I pay? +

By Visa or Mastercard, including Apple Pay and Google Pay, or in USDT or USDC on Solana or Polygon. Your plan or API key switches on automatically as soon as the payment is confirmed.

How accurate is the scheduling? +

Posts are scheduled to the minute. A scheduler on Cloudflare’s edge checks for due posts every minute and sends them straight away, so there is no extra queue delay on our side. Each platform’s own API response time still applies.

When does Vane launch? +

We are onboarding people from the waitlist first. Join with your email and we will write to you when your spot opens.

What happens to my email? +

It is stored in our database and used only to tell you about Vane’s launch. We don’t sell or share it.

Are the demos above real? +

Both are simulations that run in your browser. The post preview and the iPad video demo do not publish anywhere, and nothing you type is sent to a server.
